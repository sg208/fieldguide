What Field Guide collects

Nothing about you. There are no accounts, no cookies, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking scripts. Field Guide does not use local storage or any other browser storage to identify you.

What your browser sends

When you search for a city or open a page, your browser asks our server at fieldguide.engg.app for it. Our server then asks the data providers on your behalf:

  • Open-Meteo receives the city name you type, to find matching places.
  • iNaturalist receives the coordinates of the city you picked, the radius, and the filters you chose, to return species, photos and sightings.

These requests come from our server, not from your browser, so the providers do not see your IP address. Photos are passed through our server too.

Hosting

Field Guide runs on Cloudflare. Like any host, Cloudflare processes your IP address and basic request details to deliver pages and protect the site from abuse. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy.

Saved on your device

Field Guide is an installable web app. Its service worker keeps a copy of the app and of pages you recently opened in your browser’s cache, so they load fast and work offline. This cache stays on your device and is never sent to us. Clear it any time by clearing site data for fieldguide.engg.app in your browser settings.

Your location

Field Guide never asks for your location. You pick a city by name.

Links to other sites

Species pages link to iNaturalist and Wikipedia. Those sites have their own privacy policies once you follow a link.

Changes and contact
